Common questions

Do you need access to our PMS/EHR?

No. We don’t touch Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, or any practice management system. We work entirely outside your IT infrastructure and book into your existing online calendar.

Does our IT provider need to be involved?

Nope. Zero IT involvement — no network changes, no software installs, no access to internal systems. We handle everything on our end.

What about HIPAA compliance?

We only handle new-patient inquiries — before they’re in your system. No PHI, no chart access. We can provide a BAA if needed, though most practices don’t require one for this use case.

How long does setup take?

Most practices are live in under two weeks. We configure the system, you review and test it with us, then we flip it on.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month — cancel with 30 days’ notice. No long-term contracts.

What if we already have an answering service?

Traditional answering services take messages — we actually book the appointment. We close the loop so your team doesn’t play phone tag the next day.
